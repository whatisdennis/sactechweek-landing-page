Sac Tech Week 2026 runs October 19 – 24, citywide. This application is for technology companies, research labs, civic organizations, and creative collectives working in AI, automation, civic innovation, GovTech, MedTech, HealthTech, and robotics that want their event, exhibition, or project in the official STW program and guide.

How it works

  • 01 / When

    Events, pitch competitions, and lectures must take place during the week — October 19 – 24, 2026.

  • 02 / Open

    Every STW event is accessible to the general public — free, reserved, or ticketed. STW doesn't run individual event registration.

  • 03 / Yours

    Participants produce their own exhibition, project, or event. You build it; the week amplifies it.

  • 04 / Press

    Images you submit may be used to promote the week.

Participants receive

  • 01

    A listing and page on the STW site, linking to yours.

  • 02

    A listing in the official STW '26 Guide.

  • 03

    Inclusion in STW social posts, press releases, and potential coverage in local press and beyond.

  • 04

    Placement in STW newsletters reaching a targeted technology and innovation audience.

  • 05

    An audience actively discovering and scaling technology across the Greater Sacramento region.

Fees support the administration and marketing of the week. Need financial assistance to apply? Email hello@sactechweek.org.
