Put your event
on the map.

Sac Tech Week 2026 runs October 19 – 24, citywide. Choose from five ways to participate: host an event, show work through projection mapping or the AI Film Festival, step up as a summit speaker, or bring your company to the Startup Marketplace.

Participants receive

  • 01

    A listing and page on the STW site, linking to yours.

  • 02

    A listing in the official STW '26 Guide.

  • 03

    Inclusion in STW social posts, press releases, and potential coverage in local press and beyond.

  • 04

    Placement in STW newsletters reaching a targeted technology and innovation audience.

  • 05

    An audience actively discovering and scaling technology across the Greater Sacramento region.

Have a question? Reach out to hello@sactechweek.org.
