Sac Tech Week isn't staged by an events company. It's produced by the city's own ecosystem — the venues, communities, makers, and studios that do the work year-round. One week a year, they point in the same direction.

  • [ 01 / Community ]

    Sacramento's startup community hub — connecting founders to programming, mentorship, and each other across the capital region.

  • [ 02 / Science + Curiosity ]

    The museum where the city meets science — anchoring STW's civic and educational reach.

  • [ 03 / The Marketplace Floor ]

    The workspace community hosting the Startup Marketplace — 12,000 square feet of booth-style showcase at the heart of the Summit.

  • [ 04 / Makers ]

    Champions of Sacramento-made goods and the maker economy — proof the region builds things you can hold.

  • [ 05 / Creative Innovation ]

    A creative innovation space where art, sustainability, and technology share a roof.

  • [ 06 / Knowledge + Culture ]

    Ecosystem partner carrying ideas between the region's builders, thinkers, and audiences.

  • [ 07 / Operations ]

    The virtual-assistant team that keeps a decentralized, citywide week running on time.

  • [ 08 / Design + Technology ]

    The design and technology studio behind STW's identity and digital presence.
