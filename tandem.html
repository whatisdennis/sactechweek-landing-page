  • Human + technology

We go further when humans and technology work together.

The Tandem Principle

Human first doesn't mean technology last.

It means putting human needs, agency, dignity and the health of our planet into the conversation from the beginning—not after the technology has been built.

+  The Conversation — six tracks

Not a traditional tech conference. An exploration of capability, humanity and Earth.

01

Human

How do we stay human?

  • What should never be automated?
  • Are we outsourcing our thinking?
  • What should remain human?
02

Human + Machine

What does it mean to work in Tandem?

  • AI as collaborator, not replacement
  • The augmented worker
  • What humans are uniquely good at
03

Society

How do we live together?

  • Who decides what technology should do?
  • Trust in a synthetic world
  • Whose values get encoded?
04

Build

What are we actually creating?

  • Designing for agency, not addiction
  • Accessibility and privacy by design
  • Human-in-the-loop technology
05

Earth

  • AI, energy and water
  • E-waste and critical minerals
  • Can technology become regenerative?
06

Future

  • 2035: a day in the life
  • What should we preserve?
  • What would a good future look like?
