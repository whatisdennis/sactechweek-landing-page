04 / WHY YOU SHOULD SHOW UP

Six days. Twenty-plus reasons to leave the house.

20+ events across the city — panels, workshops, build sessions, mixers, and demos. Most run 1–2 hours. 4 signature events hosted by STW. 1 STW Tandem Summit to close it out — 30,000 sq ft, 500+ people, the whole ecosystem in one room.

Most of it is free. All of it is open. You don't need a badge, an invite, or a job in tech.

  • 01 / You’ll meet the people building here

    Not the ones speaking about it. Founders mid-pitch, engineers mid-build, makers mid-process. In the room, not on a stage.

  • 02 / You’ll see it before it’s finished

    Products that aren’t done yet. A warehouse turned into light and motion. Films made by people who live twenty minutes from you.

  • 03 / You’ll be early

    To whatever Sacramento becomes next. This is year one.

No badge scanners. No hotel ballrooms. No velvet rope. The city is the venue and the door is open.

05 / THE PROGRAM

Four signature events. One Summit. Twenty-plus more across the city.

Four events hosted by STW anchor the week, and the STW Tandem Summit closes it. Around them, partners run their own events in every corner of Sacramento — same week, all over town.

  1. [ 01 / STW ] Film × AI Urban Hive / Premieres at the Summit

    AI Film Lab

    A curated short-film showcase: twelve AI filmmakers and twelve youth creators from the AI Film Lab Summer Program, exploring storytelling where creativity meets artificial intelligence.

    Submit Your Film ↗
  2. [ 02 / STW ] Showcase Urban Hive / 12,000 sq ft

    Startup Marketplace

    The central showcase of the STW Tandem Summit — product-ready startups from across the ecosystem in a booth-style floor highlighting the builders, tools, and ideas shaping the region's future.

    Startup Signup Coming Soon ↗
  3. [ 03 / STW ] Projection art Urban Hive / Free + open to the public

    B.Y.O.B.

    Bring Your Own Beamer — a projection-mapping showcase from multiple artists, turning space into a living canvas of light and motion. Twice daily over three days, with live music, up to 200 guests per showing.

    Artist Signup ↗
  4. [ 04 / STW ] Opening Government × academia × industry

    Kickoff Breakfast

    Sacramento State of Innovation — the week opens with leaders across government, academia, and industry, and keynote insights on the state of innovation in the region.

  5. [ 05 / STW ] Grand finale Urban Hive / 30,000+ sq ft / 500+ attendees

    STW Tandem Summit

    The grand finale: a full-day exploration of what's next, with workshops, panels, live demonstrations, fifteen-plus activations, and a marketplace of emerging ventures.
