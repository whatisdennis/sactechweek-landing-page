              04 / WHY YOU SHOULD SHOW UP

              Six days. Twenty-plus reasons to leave the house.

              20+ events across the city — panels, workshops, build sessions, mixers, and demos. Most run 1–2 hours. Five signature events hosted by STW, with the Tandem Summit closing out the week.

              Most of it is free. All of it is open. You don't need a badge, an invite, or a job in tech.

              • 01 / You’ll meet the people building here

                Not the ones speaking about it. Founders mid-pitch, engineers mid-build, makers mid-process. In the room, not on a stage.

              • 02 / You’ll see it before it’s finished

                Products that aren’t done yet. A warehouse turned into light and motion. Films made by people who live twenty minutes from you.

              • 03 / You’ll be early

                To whatever Sacramento becomes next. This is year one.

              No badge scanners. No hotel ballrooms. No velvet rope. The city is the venue and the door is open.
