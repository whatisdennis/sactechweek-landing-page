Follow the white rabbit ↓

Not a conference. A city-scale technology ecosystem.

One week. One city. Infinite possibilities. Sac Tech Week is a decentralized, citywide celebration of innovation — founders, startups, technologists, creatives, investors, students, and community leaders across the capital region.

Workshops, panels, demos, art experiences, startup showcases, and gatherings hosted throughout Sacramento. No badge scanners. No hotel ballrooms. The city is the venue.

  • 01 / The City

    Local businesses, warehouses, museums. Sacramento hosts the week.

  • 02 / The Builders

    Built by people in the middle of it. Founders, designers, engineers, ecosystem builders.

  • 03 / The Door

    Open to anyone curious. No badge tiers, no velvet rope. Show up and follow the rabbit.

★ Five signature events ★

One week.
One city. ★
Infinite signal.

Five signature events anchor the week, hosted by STW. Around them, consortium partners run satellite events across the city — same week, every corner of Sacramento. Full calendar coming soon.

  1. [ 01 / STW ] Film × AI Urban Hive / Premieres at the Summit

    AI Film Lab

    A curated short-film showcase: twelve AI filmmakers and twelve youth creators from the AI Film Lab Summer Program, exploring storytelling where creativity meets artificial intelligence.

  2. [ 02 / STW ] Showcase Urban Hive / 12,000 sq ft

    Startup Marketplace

    The central showcase of the Capitol Region Innovation Summit — product-ready startups from across the ecosystem in a booth-style floor highlighting the builders, tools, and ideas shaping the region's future.

  3. [ 03 / STW ] Projection art Urban Hive / Free + open to the public

    B.Y.O.B.

    Bring Your Own Beamer — a projection-mapping showcase from multiple artists, turning space into a living canvas of light and motion. Twice daily over three days, with live music, up to 200 guests per showing.

  4. [ 04 / STW ] Opening Government × academia × industry

    Kickoff Breakfast

    Sacramento State of Innovation — the week opens with leaders across government, academia, and industry, and keynote insights on the state of innovation in the region.

  5. [ 05 / STW ] Grand finale Urban Hive / 30,000+ sq ft / 500+ attendees

    Capitol Region Innovation Summit

    The grand finale: a full-day exploration of what's next, with workshops, panels, live demonstrations, fifteen-plus activations, and a marketplace of emerging ventures.
